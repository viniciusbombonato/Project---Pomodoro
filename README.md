#### Description:
This is a simple Pomodoro Timer web app to help you stay focused and productive. With this app, you can set how long you want to work, how long your short breaks should be, and even customize your longer breaks after a few sessions. 

Once you start, the timer will guide you through cycles of work and rest, giving you alerts when it’s time to switch. The goal is to make it easier to manage your time, avoid burnout, and get more done—one Pomodoro at a time!
